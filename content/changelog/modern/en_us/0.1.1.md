### Bug fixes
- Fixed a bug where output ingots could come from different mods (or maybe not, but I tested it and everything seemed fine).
- Hid unused TFC fluids and items.
- Hid many vanilla items, though not all of them yet.
- Fixed decrafting TFC powder into a metal that has a lot of metal units.
- Fixed sand and glass recipes.
- Fixed a bug where a TFC vein of sapphires and emeralds could generate in the world.
- Added cobble and raw stone recipes to the RockBreaker.
- Added new TFC SandStone recipes; the vanilla ones are hidden.
- Restored vanilla textures for pause menu buttons.
- Fixed a bug where TFC gems could be obtained from sluicing.
- Added decrafting of all TFC wooden items into wood dust in GT.
- Fixed the creosote board recipe.
- Fixed a bug where some unwanted AdAstra recipes could be crafted.
- Restored the MCW AIO recipes. Yes, they're janky, but I'm not doing all the work for the author — let him fix his crap mod himself.
- Fixed the TFC blast furnace recipe.
