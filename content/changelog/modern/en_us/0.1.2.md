### Bug fixes
- Fixed a bug where TFC powder could be smelted as dust.
- Improved the layout of recipes in the scripts.
- Fixed a bug where the miner's pickaxe didn't work.
- Fixed a bug where sticks could be made from boards without a saw, which produced broken stick recipes.
- Fixed recipes for small pieces of metal — nothing changed for the user, but recipe generation is now automatic.
- General code readability improvements.
- Added a few missing TFC crusher recipes (related to powder).
- Changed the LV Machine Casing recipe; it now uses red and blue steel plates.
