- The following is now available in HEI:
  - Viewing history: the ingredient you were viewing for a recipe will be displayed in the lower right corner.
  - Recipe bookmarking: when you add a recipe output to an HEI bookmark, the current recipe will be recorded, and opening the recipe via the HEI bookmark will open the recorded recipe.
  - Recipe preview: hold Ctrl to display a recipe preview in the recipe bookmark.
  - Recipe transfer: you can press ctrl + w to easily transfer a recipe from the recipe bookmark.
  - Bookmark picker: allows the player to use the middle mouse button to pick a bookmark and move it to another position.
- Added a recipe for the ender pouch.
- Ender chests, tanks, and pouches are now linked only within one dimension, but now have a larger capacity of 3x9 -> 6x9.
- The diamond "lock" on ender items now prevents other players from changing the color of your chest and prevents other players from copying colors to an ender pouch.
- A mechanic similar to the soil nutrient mechanic from 1.7.10 TFC is now in effect, with some changes. Each plant has its own favorite nutrient N, P, or K; you can check this by pressing Shift while viewing the corresponding tooltip. Once the player reaches the "Adept" skill level in agriculture, pressing shift while holding a hoe and looking at a block shows the nutrient value in that particular block.
  - Mechanics and details:
    - Each crop consumes all of its favorite nutrients from the soil to grow from 0% to maturity.
    - Farmland will passively gain about 3.14% of each nutrient every TFC month. This means it takes 32 in-game months to go from 0% to 100%.
      - If, for example, a crop that uses nutrient N is planted, then that patch of soil will not regenerate nutrient N.
        - This makes it viable to split land into 3 (or more) parts and rotate crops depending on their favorite nutrient each season in regions with a temperate climate.
        - In TFC, each crop takes ~4 months to mature.
    - A mature crop will still consume nutrients, so make sure you harvest it.
    - A dead crop left on a patch of land will reduce passive nutrient growth by 20%.
    - Fertilizers exist for each nutrient; when used, they increase its specific nutrient value by 50%.
      - The item called "Fertilizer" in TFC is now the fertilizer for nutrient K, saltpeter is the nutrient N one, and "Fertilizer (P)" is for nutrient P.
      - Type "fertilizer" in HEI and you can check all three of them.
- Changed the smoke and steam texture. It is now like in 1.18

You can access the items by right-clicking the corpse. Once you take out all the items, the corpse will disappear. After 1 hour the corpse will turn into a skeleton, indicating that it has existed for longer. This does not change the corpse's functionality.
