### Changes
- Fixed a server pack crash caused by Bansoukou.
- TFC and almost all of its addons have been merged into TerraFirmaGreg-Core; all of their functionality will gradually be rewritten and implemented in TerraFirmaGreg-Core. So far, EasyBreedingTFC, TFC-Florae-Fixes, TFC-TowerHeat, TFCAlloyCalc and TFC-Alcohols have been fully merged. If an addon included blocks, items or substances, they are automatically converted to the new ones.
- Fixed the AE Formation Core recipe — you now get 1 core instead of 2.
- Fixed a bug where anything could be put into the alloy calculator — now only items that have metal units can be inserted.
- Fixed a translation bug involving the saw forms.
- Added the Modernity texture pack as the default.
- Removed a dependency and fixed a crash that occurred when spring arrived.
