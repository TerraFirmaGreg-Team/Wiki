### Modpack fixes
- Disabled the "Force New Location" and "Auto Switch Tools" options
- Removed the Stargate mod; there is no point in having it in the modpack without the GT GCYS addon for now. If someone wants to use it, you can install it; the scripts remain.
- Replaced the planet research mod.
- Disabled automatic Optifine downloading in the CurseForge version; it may increase the FPS count on weak PCs.
- Many small optimizations.
