### Modpack fixes
- Replaced the Discord RPC mod.
- Removed optimization and bugfix mods, as they were implemented into UniversalTweaks.
- Fixed an experience duplication bug after death.
- Fixes some edge cases where fire damage sources did not make mobs drop cooked items.
- Allows skeletons to shoot flaming arrows when they are on fire (70% chance)
- If entities have fire resistance, they are extinguished immediately when burning.
- Allows fire to spread from entity to entity (30% chance)
- Easy Breeding: allows easy animal breeding by tossing food on the ground.
- Finite Water: prevents creating infinite water sources outside ocean and river biomes.
- Husk & Stray Spawning: allows husks and strays to spawn underground like regular zombies and skeletons.
- Load Sounds: plays sounds when loading the game or a world.
- No Potion Shift: disables the inventory shift when potion effects are active.
- No Redstone Lighting: disables lighting of active redstone, repeaters, and comparators to improve performance.
- No Saddled Wandering: stops horses from wandering when they are saddled.
- Player Speed: configurable player flight and walking speed.
- "Smooth Scrolling": adds smooth scrolling to every list in the game.
- "Super Hot Torch": allows setting entities on fire by hitting them with a torch.
- Swing Through Grass: allows hitting entities through grass instead of breaking it.
- Uncap FPS: removes the hard-coded 30 fps limit on screens like the main menu.
- Added the following recipes (and their corresponding uses): (Thanks MDetonados)
  - Assembler: Circuit (23) + any Grain = Vanilla wheat — used for Thaumonomicon research;
  - Assembler: Circuit (23) + any 8 Cobblestone = Vanilla furnace — used for the Essentia Smeltery recipe;
  - Assembler: Circuit (23) + Pumpkin = Vanilla pumpkin — used to create a Snow Golem, which must be scanned for thaumonomicon research;
  - Assembler: Circuit (23) + any Ore Dictionary Dirt = Vanilla dirt — used for Thaumonomicon research;
  - Extruder: Thaumium ingot + tool shape (pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, and sword) = tool head — used for the five special Thaumcraft tools.
