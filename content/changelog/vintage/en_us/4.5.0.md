### Changes
- **Make a backup of the world before updating!**
- Support for the Extended version has ended — as of this release it will no longer receive updates. Unfortunately, I haven't found a good way to convert objects from the Extended version to the standard one. That said, you can still just load your Extended world in the standard version of the pack — it shouldn't damage the world.
- Ported over from the 1.20 version: the main menu and loading screen design.
- Significantly reduced the modpack size: 105.88 MB -> 12.3 MB (CurseForge archive).
- AE2 Network Visualizer has been replaced by the alternative in AE2 Stuff Unofficial.
- ExtraCPUs has been removed and replaced with the extended processors from NAE2.
- AEAAdditions has been removed, and almost all of its functionality is now provided by NAE2.
- Changed the OC Disk Drive recipe.
- Added a recipe for the Ultimate Encoder.
- This version includes major removals, updates, and new features, as well as minor recipe fixes and optimizations.
- There were also many small changes that I've already forgotten about.
