### Modpack fixes
- Many scripts were ported from ZenScript to GroovyScript; it is recommended to install the modpack from scratch, saving your world, map, and everything you need beforehand.
- Fixed the forging and casting recipes for chisel and propick tool heads.
- Added missing OC recipes.
- Fixed an error obtaining colored steel ingots in the steam hammer.
- Fixed the non-working T1 rocket engine recipe.
- Fixed the non-working recipe for obtaining salt from a bucket of salt.
- The armor quest now accepts armor of all materials.
- Fixed another batch of non-working AE2 recipes.
