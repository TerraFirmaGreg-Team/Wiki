### Modpack fixes
- More diagnostic info and RAM usage added to the loading screen.
- Sheep spawn increased by 30%.
- The source of the ~N~ nutrient is now GT saltpeter instead of TFC.
- Fixed a bug where some crop drops were not registered.
- Adds and fixes many missing or poorly defined Firmalife planter recipes.
- Fixed placing rice in water.
- Fixed many non-working recipes.
- Minor quest edits.
- Increased the chance of storms and cyclones.
- Fixed a capsule dupe.
- Lots of balancing in the temperature system.
- Disabled villagers on planets.
- Added a recipe for flat versions of AE interfaces from the block versions.
- Fixed the singularity quest, which accepted an engineering processor instead of the singularity itself.
- Fixed the treated plank recipe, where the creosote capsule was not consumed.
- Changed the AE processor recipe.
- Miners now dig ore chunks instead of blocks.
- Weather2 replaced with Weather2 Remastered, a maintained fork with optimizations and bug fixes.
